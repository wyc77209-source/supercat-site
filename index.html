AI Money-Saving Helper for Students

Super Cat helps college and international students find savings in their monthly spending. Type what you spend on (DoorDash, Spotify, Uber, subscriptions) and get a ranked savings plan with actionable tips.

  • ✓ Free and private — spending data stays on your phone
  • ✓ No account required — start saving immediately
  • ✓ Smart recommendations based on your location and profile
  • ✓ Track realized savings and check-ins on your device

How It Works

  1. Tell Super Cat what you spend on (food delivery, subscriptions, transport, etc.)
  2. Get an estimated monthly savings amount with a ranked action plan
  3. Follow the tips — most students save $50-200/month
  4. Mark tips as done and track your realized savings

Features

  • Bill Scanner: Snap a photo of your receipt and Super Cat finds overpay opportunities
  • Commuter Tools: Compare transit passes and find the best option for your schedule
  • My Wallet: Save favorite tips, track subscriptions, and manage reminders
  • NYC Resale Guide: Find verified secondhand stores and student deals
  • 7-Day Check-ins: Optional reminders to help you stick with savings tips

Popular Savings Categories

  • • Food delivery (DoorDash, Uber Eats) — cook at home, meal prep, student dining plans
  • • Streaming subscriptions (Spotify, Netflix) — student discounts, family plans, bundling
  • • Transportation (Uber, Lyft, parking) — transit passes, bike shares, walking routes
  • • Mobile & internet — student plans, family plans, cheaper carriers
  • • Shopping & retail — secondhand stores, student discounts, resale platforms

Student-Specific Optimizations

Super Cat knows about:

  • • NYU and UCI-specific resources and discounts
  • • International student considerations (remittances, currency exchange, travel)
  • • NYC and Irvine local deals and student perks
  • • Campus-specific transit options and commuter passes

Privacy & Data

Your spending information never leaves your device. Super Cat stores everything locally on your phone. We only track anonymous usage metrics (page views, button clicks) to improve the product — never what you type or how much you spend.
